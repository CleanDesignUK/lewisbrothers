About Lewis Brothers

Local roofing and plastering experience, built around doing the job properly.

Lewis Brothers Roofing & Plastering is a Llangefni-based contractor providing professional roofing and plastering services for homes and properties across North Wales.

Our work includes slate and tile roofing, roof repairs, fascias, soffits, leadwork and chimney restoration, alongside specialist EPDM rubber flat roofing, interior skimming, exterior plastering and machine rendering.

Based in Llangefni and serving Anglesey, Gwynedd and Conwy.
