Roofing & Plastering FAQs

Lewis Brothers provides new roofs, re-roofing, roof repairs and general roofing work across Anglesey. From our Llangefni base, we work on both roof repair and roof replacement projects for homes and properties throughout the island.

Yes. EPDM rubber flat roofing is a key Lewis Brothers service. We install, replace and repair rubber flat roofing for suitable garages, extensions and other flat roof areas across Anglesey and surrounding North Wales locations.

Yes. Lewis Brothers carries out roof repairs for leaks, damaged roofing materials, storm damage and other common roof problems. The team can assess the roof before advising what repair work is appropriate.

Yes. Lewis Brothers undertakes new roof installations and complete re-roofing as well as repair work. We can assess an existing roof and discuss whether repair or replacement is the more suitable option for the property.

Yes. Alongside roofing and flat roofing, Lewis Brothers provides internal plastering and external rendering for repairs, renovations and property improvement work across Anglesey and selected North Wales areas.

Lewis Brothers is based in Llangefni and works across Anglesey, including Holyhead and surrounding communities. Selected roofing, EPDM flat roofing, plastering and rendering projects are also undertaken in North Wales locations including Bangor, Caernarfon, Conwy, Llandudno and Colwyn Bay.
