Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Lewis Brothers Roofing and Plastering uses cookies and similar browser technologies on this website and how you can control your preferences.

Last updated: 4 October 2026

About this Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies are, why this website uses them and the choices available to you.

For information about how we use personal information more generally, please read our Privacy Policy .

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small piece of information that a website stores on your device when you visit it. Cookies can be used to remember information about your visit, such as preferences or choices you have made.

Similar technologies, including browser local storage, can also store information on your device. This policy uses the term "cookies" to include cookies and similar storage technologies where appropriate.

2. Why we use cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to:

  • remember your cookie preferences;
  • support basic website functionality;
  • maintain website security and help prevent misuse;
  • understand how visitors use the website where optional analytics have been accepted; and
  • support optional marketing technologies where these are used and you have provided the required consent.

3. Cookie preference storage used by this website

The website uses a first-party preference record called lb_cookie_consent.

Name Type Purpose Duration
lb_cookie_consent Necessary preference cookie / browser storage Remembers whether you have accepted or rejected optional analytics and marketing technologies so that the website can respect your choice. Up to 12 months

The same preference may also be saved in your browser's local storage so that your choice can be recognised reliably on future visits.

4. Types of cookies and technologies

Strictly necessary

These technologies are needed for functions requested by the visitor or for the website to operate correctly. They may include storing your cookie preference or supporting security-related functions.

Strictly necessary technologies do not require consent where the applicable legal exemption applies.

Analytics

Analytics technologies can help a website understand matters such as which pages are visited and how people interact with the website.

Where non-essential analytics technologies are used on this website, they should only be activated after you have chosen to accept optional cookies.

Marketing

Marketing technologies may be used to measure advertising or provide advertising-related features. Where such technologies are used and consent is required, they should not be activated unless you have chosen to accept them.

5. Optional cookies

The website's cookie controls distinguish between necessary functionality and optional analytics or marketing technologies.

Optional technologies that require consent should not be loaded unless your saved preference allows them. If the website's analytics or marketing tools change, this Cookie Policy should be updated to identify the relevant technology and its purpose.

6. Contact forms and Web3Forms

Website enquiry forms are processed using Web3Forms. When you actively submit a form, the information you entered is sent to Web3Forms so that the enquiry can be delivered to Lewis Brothers Roofing and Plastering.

The processing of information submitted through a contact form is explained in our Privacy Policy .

7. Third-party websites and services

This website may contain links to third-party platforms including:

  • Google Business Profile;
  • Facebook; and
  • WhatsApp.

If you follow a link to another website or service, that provider may use its own cookies and technologies. Their use is controlled by their own privacy and cookie policies rather than this Cookie Policy.

8. Your cookie choices

When the cookie banner is shown, you can choose:

  • Accept all to allow the optional categories supported by the website; or
  • Reject optional to keep only necessary functionality.

You can also remove cookies or browser storage through your browser settings. Doing so may cause the cookie banner to appear again the next time you visit.

9. Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies through their privacy or site-data settings.

Blocking all cookies may affect some website functions. Your browser's help or privacy section should explain how to manage stored website data.

10. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this policy when the website changes, when new technologies are introduced or when legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page with the updated date.
